**Description** (4000 max):

    Learn Mandarin Chinese one word at a time, and remember it. Hanzi Cards (汉字卡) brings together flashcards that come back just before you'd forget them, short stories you can tap for meanings, a full dictionary and answers you say out loud, on iPhone, iPad and your computer, with no account and nothing sent to anyone.

    FLASHCARDS THAT REMEMBER FOR YOU
    • Modern spaced repetition (FSRS-6): every word is scheduled on its own, so each session is the reviews you need today and nothing you already know
    • Four ways to study a word: characters → meaning, meaning → characters, listening, and speaking
    • Speak your answer and Apple's speech recognition checks it, right on your device
    • Pick decks, tags, session size, and what to study: due and new, new only, everything, or just the words you keep forgetting
    • Characters and pinyin coloured by tone, so tones stick from the first day
    • Starts with an HSK 1 deck and a short story written with its words, so you can begin straight away

    DECKS AND CARDS
    • Nested decks and tags; every new word is tagged with its HSK level for you
    • Type pinyin to find characters, like a keyboard (nihao → 你好), and the pinyin and meaning fill in from the dictionary

    READING
    • Stories in collections, from the starter story to your own
    • Tap any word for its pinyin and meaning, and make it a card in one tap
    • Hear a story read aloud by Apple's Chinese voice, from the beginning or from any word, with pause and resume
    • Key vocabulary for every story; mark stories as read

    DICTIONARY
    • CC-CEDICT, about 120,000 entries, on your device and offline
    • Search by characters, pinyin (with or without tones) or English
    • One tap adds a word to a deck

    STATISTICS THAT MOTIVATE
    • Time studied, reviews, words remembered against your target, and your day streak
    • Time by day, card progress from new to mature, an activity map and what's due in the coming month
    • The words you forget most, and how well you do by hour and by day of the week

    ONE LIBRARY ON EVERY DEVICE
    Hanzi Cards is also a free desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, built from the same code, and LINK keeps them all in step: your cards, decks, study progress, statistics and stories.
    • Study on the train with your iPhone; your session is waiting on your computer when you sit down
    • Syncs by itself when the app opens, when you come back to it and when you leave it
    • Changes made on several devices are merged: the latest edit of each card, every answer from every device, and each word's schedule from its most recent review
    • No account and no cloud: the shared copy lives on a computer you own (or a small server program you run yourself), reached over your Wi-Fi, your own VPN or the internet
    • Encrypted on your device before it leaves, and sent only to the computer you paired with
    • Pair once with a code from your computer and forget about it; LINK is optional, and everything else works without it

    MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
    • iPhone: one page at a time, with a side bar that steps aside while you study or read
    • iPad: the side bar always shows, lists sit beside what you're working on, in any orientation and in Split View
    • Sixteen themes, from soft and floral to calm and classic, each in light and dark
    • Siri: say "Start studying in Hanzi Cards"
    • English or Chinese interface

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your cards, history and voice never leave your devices, except to your own computer if you turn on LINK. Hanzi Cards is open source.

## App Review Information

**Sign-in required:** No (there are no accounts)

**Notes:**

    Thank you for reviewing Hanzi Cards, a Mandarin Chinese study app. There is no account, no sign-in, no purchase and no content shared between users. On first launch the app creates a sample deck (12 HSK 1 words) and a short story, so everything can be tried straight away.

    SUGGESTED WALK-THROUGH
    1. Study (first page): tap Start studying, then Show answer, then Wrong or Right. Statistics fill in as you answer.
    2. Speaking: on the Study page, under Card directions, choose "Meaning → Say it", then Start studying and say the answer when the card asks for it. iOS asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition. Apple's recognizer checks the Chinese answer on the device (on-device recognition only); audio is not stored or sent anywhere.
    3. Reading: Reading › All stories › 新朋友. Tap a word for its pinyin and meaning; tap Read aloud (the round speaker button on iPhone) to hear the story in the system's Chinese voice.
    4. Dictionary: search "xue", "ni hao" or "friend"; the + button adds a word to a deck.
    5. Decks & Cards: open Getting Started to see and edit its cards.
    6. Siri / Shortcuts: "Start studying in Hanzi Cards" opens the app and starts a session.
    7. iPad: the side bar stays visible; the app supports every orientation and Split View.

    PERMISSIONS
    • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only for spoken answers (step 2), on the device.
    • Local Network: only for LINK, below. Nothing else on the network is contacted.

    LINK (OPTIONAL SYNC WITH THE USER'S OWN COMPUTER)
    Settings › LINK keeps the user's own cards and progress the same on their devices. Hanzi Cards is also a free desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux; github.com/wjhdfnmf/hanzi-cards). The desktop app, or a small server program the user runs, holds the shared copy; the iPhone or iPad is always a client. There is no server run by the developer and no account. To pair, the computer shows an address and a pairing code, which are entered on the phone. Because pairing needs the user's own computer, it cannot be completed from the device alone; the attached screen recording shows pairing and a sync from start to finish. Everything else in the app works without LINK.

    ENCRYPTION
    LINK uses standard algorithms only (TLS 1.3, XChaCha20-Poly1305) to protect the user's own data between their own devices. Encryption is not the app's purpose, so the app is declared exempt (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).

    CONTENT AND PRIVACY
    • Dictionary: CC-CEDICT, CC BY-SA 4.0, credited in the app (Dictionary page and Settings › About).
    • The starter deck and story were written for the app.
    • No data is collected; no analytics, advertising or tracking. Privacy policy: github.com/wjhdfnmf/hanzi-cards/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
