声调录音表Tone recording sheet

谢谢你帮忙！我在做一个学中文的应用，想让它能听出学生的声调对不对。现在测试用的只有电脑合成的声音，所以需要真人母语者的录音。你只要照着下面的表读出来，用手机录音就可以。 Thank you for helping! I'm building an app for learning Chinese and want it to hear whether a learner's tones are right. So far it has only been tested on a synthetic voice, so it needs recordings of a real native speaker. You read from the sheet below and record yourself on your phone.

  • 29 组，每组约 10 个。29 blocks of about 10 items each.
  • 每组约 1 分钟。About 1 minute per block.
  • 共约 40 分钟，可分两次录。About 40 minutes in all, and you can split it into two sittings.

准备Before you start

  • 找一个安静的房间：关掉电视、音乐和风扇，关上窗户。A quiet room: TV, music and fans off, window closed.
  • 用手机自带的录音应用。iPhone 用「语音备忘录」，先在 设置 → App → 语音备忘录 → 音质 里选「无损」。Android 用「录音机」，有高音质选项就选高音质。Use the phone's own recorder. iPhone: Voice Memos, and first set Settings → Apps → Voice Memos → Audio Quality to Lossless. Android: the Recorder app, on its highest quality.
  • 把手机平放在桌上，离嘴 20–30 厘米，录音时不要拿着手机，整个过程手机位置不变。Lay the phone flat on a table 20–30 cm from your mouth. Don't hold it, and keep it in the same place throughout.
  • 在电脑、平板上打开这一页，或者打印出来，照着读。Read this page on a computer or tablet, or print it.

每一组怎么录Recording one block

  1. 开始录音，先说组号，比如「第3组」。Start recording and say the block number, e.g. “dì 3 zǔ”.
  2. 停两秒，然后按顺序（从左到右，一行一行）把每一个读一遍。Pause two seconds, then say each item once, in order (left to right, row by row).
  3. 每两个之间停大约两秒（心里数「一、二」）。电脑靠停顿来分开每个词。Pause about two seconds between items (count “one, two” in your head). The computer uses the pauses to cut the recording apart.
  4. 读完最后一个，停两秒，结束录音。勾上「录好了」。After the last one, wait two seconds and stop. Tick “录好了”.

读错了、咳嗽了或者有别的声音？不要在中间补读，删掉这个录音，整组重新录。一组只要一分钟。Misread, coughed, or a noise? Don't repeat the item inside the recording: delete it and record the whole block again. A block takes a minute.

怎么读How to say them

  • 用你平时的普通话，自然地说，不要故意夸张声调。Your normal Mandarin, said naturally. Don't exaggerate the tones.
  • 每个字、每个词都单独说，像在教学生，而不是像念一串清单（念清单时最后一个常常会变调）。Say each item on its own, as if teaching a student, not like reading a list (the end of a list tends to change its pitch).
  • 第一轮（第 1–16 组）：清楚地说，像老师给学生示范，但不要拖长或夸张。包括字、词、故意读错声调的词（第 14 组开始）和几个句子。Pass 1 (blocks 1–16): clear, like a teacher's example, but not drawn out or exaggerated. It has syllables, words, words with wrong tones on purpose (from block 14) and a few sentences.
  • 第二轮（第 17–29 组）：同样的字和词，换了顺序，用平时说话的速度，像跟朋友聊天。最好休息一下再录，换一天更好。两轮的说法不一样正是需要的，不用刻意让它们一样。Pass 2 (blocks 17–29): the same syllables and words in a new order, at everyday speed, as you'd say them to a friend. Take a break first, or record it on another day. It's fine, and useful, if the two passes sound different.
  • 拼音只是提示，颜色表示声调：一声、二声、三声、四声、轻声。词里的变调（比如「你好」）照你平时的说法就行。The pinyin is only a hint; its colour shows the tone. Say tone changes such as 你好 the way you normally do.

第一轮：清楚地说Pass 1: clear speech · blocks 1–16

像老师示范一样，清楚但自然。Clear but natural, like a teacher's example.

第 14 组开始只有拼音，声调是故意写错的，比如 lāoshī。请照着拼音的声调读，就算听起来很奇怪也没关系。这是在模仿外国学生的错误，用来测试应用能不能听出来。From block 14 there is only pinyin, with tones that are wrong on purpose, e.g. lāoshī. Say the tone that is written, even if it sounds odd: it imitates a learner's mistake, to test whether the app hears it.
